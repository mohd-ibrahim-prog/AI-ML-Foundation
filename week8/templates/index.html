This is an AI-based plant disease classification demonstration built for the AI/ML Foundation Program. It is a CNN trained on the PlantVillage dataset (38 classes) and is not a substitute for advice from an agricultural expert. Results are most reliable for clear, single-leaf photos similar to the dataset.
